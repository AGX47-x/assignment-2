The **Vole machine** is a tiny, fake computer built to help students learn how real computers work. Instead of using complex modern chips, it uses a very simple setup with 16 storage spots (called registers), a small 256-character memory, and a basic brain (the CPU) to process math and instructions. It reads short instructions one by one to load numbers, do simple math, and save the results, making it easy to understand the basics of computer hardware and code.
